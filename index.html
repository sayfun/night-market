Soi Nam Chai · Local news

After three floods in two years, the district wants to fix the soi's drainage.

31stalls damaged by the last flood
9days the market was shut

The last flood damaged 31 stalls and shut the market for nine days.

"The next flood could come on a night with 3,000 people in the soi. We can't promise it won't."

District engineer Somchai Pradit

Fire inspectors also found blocked exits at nine stalls this year. All were fixed after warnings.

The plan: close the market for 18 months, rebuild the drainage and widen the road. Vendors are offered a temporary site 2 km away. A vendor petition against the closure has 3,200 signatures.

Soi Nam Chai · Local news

140 stalls, 22 years, and an 18-month closure.

40%fewer customers expected at the temporary site
25stalls lost to the wider road

"My rent is paid by the evenings. Eighteen months isn't a break. It's the end."

Malee Wongsa, grilled-pork vendor since the market opened in 2004

About 60 of the vendors live in the neighbourhood. The district offers a temporary site 2 km away, but its own estimate is that it will draw about 40% fewer customers.

After the market reopens, vendors keep their stall rights, but about 25 stalls would be lost to the wider road.

Of 20 nearby shops surveyed, 12 say the market's foot traffic is important to their business. A petition against the closure has 3,200 signatures. The district says the closure is needed after three floods in two years.

Half the market's visitors are under 30. It could go dark for 18 months.

A university study counted about 1,800 visitors on a weekday evening and 3,100 at weekends. About half were under 30.

"It's where we eat after class and where my friends actually meet. I don't know where we'd go instead."

Nok, 20, university student

"A night market is how a neighbourhood remembers itself."

Dr. Anan, local historian

The district plans an 18-month closure after three floods in two years. A temporary site 2 km away is offered. Some neighbours complain about noise and litter: 6 of 20 nearby shops surveyed. A petition against the closure has 3,200 signatures.

Soi Nam Chai · Local news

The district has a drainage budget that must be committed by the end of the fiscal year.

40%fewer customers expected at the temporary site
25stalls lost to the wider road

"If we don't commit the money by year end, it goes to another district. We won't get it back."

Pim Charoen, district finance officer

The plan: close the market for 18 months, rebuild the drainage after three floods in two years, and widen the road. About 25 stalls would be lost to the wider road. A temporary site 2 km away is expected to draw about 40% fewer customers.

The vendor association has asked for a phased closure instead. The district says phased works would cost more and take longer.

Of 20 nearby shops surveyed, 12 say the market matters to their business, 6 complain of noise and litter, and 2 have no opinion. A petition against the closure has 3,200 signatures.
